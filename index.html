About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at Case Western Reserve University, expected to graduate in August 2027. My research aims to build an end-to-end generalizable AI framework for industrial automation and robotic systems in three connected stages:

1 · Learn

Scalable and interpretable representations from large-scale data.

Self-supervised learning, interpretable AI

2 · Deploy

ML models across edge–cloud architectures.

Edge AI, IIoT

3 · Evolve

Continuously, as unseen data and conditions emerge after deployment.

Continual learning, federated learning

I worked on site at General Motors for six months, validating my pretext-training framework for inline laser-welding monitoring and delivering it to internal customers in manufacturing. The framework is now being adapted to more manufacturing processes. I am also working to commercialize EdgeSense, an edge–cloud AI system for industrial monitoring developed from my research.

Research Projects

Feature-guided Interpretable Continual Learning Across Data Modalities

Ongoing Ph.D. research

Developing an interpretable training framework that helps the model disentangle concepts from the data, so it can identify and protect true knowledge while adapting to out-of-distribution data. Expected to generalize to both time-series and image data.

Continual Learning Interpretable AI Out-of-Distribution Time-Series & Vision

Interpretability Analysis of Vision-Language-Action Robot Policies

NeurIPS 2026 Main Track

Investigated the internal mechanisms of six VLA models (80M–7B parameters) with interpretability methods across 394,000+ rollout episodes on four benchmarks. Showed that visual representations strongly influence action generation and that learned motor programs are tied to scene coordinates rather than task semantics. Released Action Atlas, an interactive explorer of the learned representations.

Robotics VLA Models Mechanistic Interpretability Causal Interventions

On-Device Continual Self-Supervised Learning for Machine Condition Monitoring

IEEE Internet of Things Journal (in revision); U.S. provisional patent

Designed an edge-computing node for real-time condition monitoring of rotating machinery. Implemented continual self-supervised learning entirely on the microcontroller within 3 MB of memory, without cloud-based model updates.

Edge Computing On-Device Training Continual Learning IIoT

Hierarchical Self-Supervised Learning from Unlabeled Industrial Data

J. Manufacturing Systems 2025; J. Manufacturing Processes 2026

Developed multi-level contrastive learning frameworks (MoCo- and DINO-style) to learn from unlabeled factory data for both welding images and multichannel photodiode signals. Combining labeled lab data with unlabeled plant data improved transfer to defect classification and weld-quality prediction.

Self-Supervised Learning Contrastive Learning MoCo DINO

Transfer Learning for Virtual Process Sensing in Resistance Spot Welding

Manufacturing Letters 2025

Co-developed a transfer-learning-enhanced Transformer that estimates in-process weld quantities from available signals, serving as a digital twin that reduces the need for direct measurement.

Transformer Transfer Learning Virtual Sensing Digital Twin
